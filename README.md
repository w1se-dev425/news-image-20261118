Sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026's world news in one image: a newspaper front page. Lead story: the Kerch Bridge is hit for a second night as the Trump–Xi–Putin summit nears, with an AI-generated editorial illustration of the damaged sea bridge. Also on the page: the U.S. sinks five Iranian tankers, the U.S. loses measles-free status, a Russian military plane crash kills 19, and COP31 enters its decisive phase — plus a briefs strip (Ebola cases more than double in Congo, Cyclone Xavier hits the Cook Islands, GTA VI launches tomorrow). The program copies the bundled front page (`entry.png`) to `/output/entry.png`, so it is deterministic and offline: two runs write identical bytes.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
